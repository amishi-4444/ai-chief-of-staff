# Problem Analysis: AI Chief of Staff

## Business context
A CEO of a fast-growing company loses hours every day to inbox triage, meeting prep, ad-hoc
research, tracking goals and chasing follow-ups. Critical items (an investor deadline, an
incident postmortem) get buried among low-value mail, and strategic goals drift unnoticed.

## Stakeholders
- **CEO** (primary user): wants fewer decisions on trivia, clear priorities, full control of critical actions.
- **Executive team** (CFO, CTO, COO): receive timely follow-ups and decisions.
- **Investors / board / partners**: expect fast, accurate responses.

## Problem statement
Build a multi-agent executive assistant that manages email, prepares meeting briefs, conducts
research, prioritises daily tasks, monitors strategic goals, coordinates follow-ups and
generates executive summaries, with human approval for critical actions.

## Objectives
1. Cut daily admin time via a one-command morning briefing.
2. Never send an email or book a meeting without explicit human approval.
3. Surface goals that are behind schedule before they become crises.
4. Give meeting-ready briefs grounded in internal knowledge.
5. Keep a full audit trail of every agent action.
